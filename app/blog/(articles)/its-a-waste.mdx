title: "Don't do it"
date: "2026-03-27"
summary: "It's a waste of time and no one wants your help."
---

Honestly I don't know why I ever bothered.

Right now I'm questioning why did I waste so much time at a university and I've come to the conclusion that I should be drinking at a beer garden.

Whoever convinced me that these philosophers who talk about defeating nihilism through responsibility to others were lying.

There's truly no reward.

If you're a young man, my advice is don't go to university. No one wants your help, companies don't want to hire you. Just find some friends who have the smallest drug habit possible and laugh your life away. Do the least amount of work possible to pay your rent and buy beer. The rest is a lie.

I've accomplished nothing, made no money, I have no job offers and I haven't seen my friends in years.

Everyone would have been so much happier if I'd had just stuck around, and what do I have to look forward to as a consequence of my sacrifice? Never earning enough money to retire and buy property or holiday.

What's changed before and after?

---------

I hate to draw a connection, but the feeling I have now is reminiscent of the feeling a young man feels when he first sees pornography.

The nice attractive ladies you might wish to meet are selling themselves to strangers for a profit and thrill, and you're just a spectator.

An unhappy, lonely and unfulfilled spectator.

People will tell you that experiences are what matters and you'll find the right person, but even after 28 years I feel like a stranger in my own land.

----------

In every other generation people were established by now. Why am I delaying gratification until I'm 60 to own fuck all in a city I don't like full of strangers.
