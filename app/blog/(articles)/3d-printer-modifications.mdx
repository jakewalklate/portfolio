In 2023, I found a cheap Wanhao i3 mini 3D printer listed on Facebook marketplace. They were selling these at Aldi Australia and people started reselling them really cheap.

The printer was fine, but suffered from the same problems the Ender 3 had, but things had developed within the community. Prusa had made waves with their realiability and automatic adjustments for the print bed's height differences. Instead of manually conmfiguring it every print you set it to something good enough and software would auto-correct the printer.

People had ported the idea of auto-bed levelling to other printers, so I decided to do the same thing. I bought a BL-Touch sensor and got to work.

The first thing you need to do is replace the motherboard, since the default board has no header pins available for peripherals you needed to work around that. One way to do that is to flash the firmware on the motherboard to become just a USB passthrough and control it via a Raspberry Pi. So I flashed the firmware on the printer and installed Klipper and Mainsail onto my raspberry pi. 

I then printed a bed extension so I could have a larger area to print on. I bought some steel rods from a hardware store and polished them, then put the new bed ontop of the extended print area with a new GT2 pulley and configured the software.

<center><img src="/printer-upgrade/n4.png" width="60%"/></center>

<br />

It looked like a frankenstein, but it was functional and printed parts without burning the house down. But I quickly became frustrated with how slow it was, mainly due to the construction of it, if it accelerated too quickly the frame shook and the print became unstable.

This was quite a significant modification. It involved soldering, running new wires through the printer, flashing firmware, writing linux config files but gave me wireless control of the printer via a LAN connected web UI and webcam monitoring.

But after spending hours modifying the printer, I scrapped it and took the motors out of it, kept everything that was useful and threw the rest in the recycling.

In 2025 my wife offered to buy me another 3D printer. Bambu Labs had hit the scene and revolutionised the printing world, they were now extremely reliable, looked good, were quite and user friendly. You no longer had to be an obsessed hacker to own one.

I went to Jaycar looking for a cheap alternative and was suggested the Anycubic Kobra S1. I bought it and took it home and was amazed that it printed such high quality parts.

Anycubic had copied Bambu Lab's formula, but done it well. The hardware is exceptional, and they weren't a newcomer, they had been also making printers similar to the Ender 3 pro, what would be called _clones_ in printing.

One of the coolest projects (in my opinion) that I printed was the [International Space Station orbit tracker](International Space Station orbit tracker) which you can read about on this website by following the link.

I used it for about a hundred or so printing hours, but then suddenly started getting bugs. The operating system would freeze, become unrecoverable and I couldn't use the printer at all.

I emailed Anycubic's support, and they ignored me. I searched forums and asked for help, and found out Anycubic are notorious for having bad customer support. And I couldn't find anyone else who had the same issue as me.

Someone asked me "did you install Rinkhals by any chance?". I googled Rinkhals and decided I didn't know what it was so I moved on.

I asked Claude where I should start, Claude suggested Rinkhals might actually be able to help, so I installed it and we gained SSH access into the printer.

The process in a matter of 16 seconds went from 55MB to 161MB of memory usage, then the linux kernel panicked and killed the process becuase the rockchip SoC was out of RAM.

Then Claude had an idea, perhaps it's the network connectivity, it wrote a script to modify the startup sequence and disabled networking but then also inserted a script with an infinite loop to monitor it without sleeping. I ran it without checking and everythign broke and the printer refused to exit the loop, it's a single core machine and the loop process was hogging it all.

Now what? My printer UI doesn't work, and we disabled network connectivity, how the hell do I ssh into the printer now?

At this point, Claude started hallucinating and gave up, it apologised to me for bricking the printer and it had no answers. It told me things that don't exist, methods it was lying about, trying to be helpful but it was at the end of it's knowledge.

I searched GitHub for a firmware backup to see what scripts were available for me, perhaps a USB peripheral could help me.

I went to the Rinkhals discord community and asked them for help, they told me I could enter MASKROM mode.

MASKROM is the last line of defence against being bricked. By the grace of god the engineers at Rockchip created a mode to bypass the BIOS and receive instructions directly from USB, and Anycubic had made it available on my motherboard.

The developers on discord had created scripts to dump the NAND flash, but they didn't seem to even remember they had done it. I asked for help and they were clueless, then I searched the chat and found they had already discussed modifying the memory via UART and header pins.

Pretty standard I guess, just a days work for them, they probably do a hundred things per day.

I downloaded their [Rockchip backup restore wizard](https://github.com/Kobra-S1/rockchip_backup_restore_wizard) and was able to extract the eMMC contents, then from within a Linux Mint PC USB BOOT I was able to repair by hand all of the broken contents of the memory segments and remove the scripts Claude had added.

I overwrote the mount drives by plugging in a UART USB cable and holding the SW1 button on the circuit board to enable the wizard to function, the rockchip ARM CPU follows a very specific disk reading protocol. First it looks at the env partition, then the uboot partition and then loads the kernel. This was all fine, my issue was within the app partition and user partition where we had a broken binary.

The printer was back to life, but the firmware was still broken?

After the network driver inits the network interface eth0, suddenly the printer starts spamming the cloud API and _essentially DoSing itself_.

I disabled networking, and with a fresh firmware partition the printer worked fine, offline at least.

So I went searching the Anycubic API to find out what was wrong. I hypothesises what might be causing this response from the API that is crashing my printer.

The reason was... my address.... Yes.. I live in an apartment, so my address is X/Y Street.

The slash wasn't escaped and was killing the API response, causing my printer to freeze by creating an infinite loop and writing memoory and result in it's own process being killed.

I went into the Anycubic settings and removed all my information, essentially wiping my account, and it works.

I have a functioning 3D printer again.

I wrote a bug report and emailed Jaycar to tell them what had happened.

Surprisingly even my USB game pad was recognised when I plugged it in, but theres no driver for it.

Fbdoom looks for input from the virtual terminal, and my gamepad sends raw HEX codes to the /dev/input/event which the kernel doesn't recognise without a driver.

So, until I have a driver the input doesn't work, but I think we can write a simple daemon that decodes the hex and pipe it to the virtual terminal, but for now I have other things to do.

After my progress, I had learnt a lot about Rinkhals, it's an awesome hack, but the problem with Rinkahls is installing it disables some of the printers default cloud functionality – nothing we couldn't fix, I just wondered why they the original developers had stoppped working on it.

I went snooping aronud the printers files and found the original programs: GkCam, GkApi, and K3SysUi.

I pulled them off the printer and opened them up in Ghidra. Rinkhals overwrites some of these files when it installs itself to give itself more functionality, these applications don't have easy APIs.

I found that Anycubic use the cloud provider called Agora, a popular Chinese host. There was already a package called _cloud2lan-bridge_ that attempted to fix the cloud problems by relaying MQTT commands, but stopped at that.

I created a program in c called _agora_pusher_. Agora pusher took the logic from GkCam and GkApi and re-wrote it in a small piece of software that pushed video streams to the Agora servers after decoding it from the hardware accelerators.

On top of that I integrated a watchdog to kill and start the process automatically, to not flood someones internet connection with useless streams.

static int is_key_frame(const uint8_t *data, size_t size) {
  if (size < 5)
    return 0;
  size_t offset = 0;
  if (data[0] == 0x00 && data[1] == 0x00) {
    if (data[2] == 0x01) {
      offset = 3;
    } else if (data[2] == 0x00 && data[3] == 0x01) {
      offset = 4;
    }
  }
  if (offset > 0 && offset < size) {
    uint8_t nalu_type = data[offset] & 0x1f;
    if (nalu_type == 5 || nalu_type == 7 || nalu_type == 8) {
      return 1;
    }
  }
  return 0;
}

// Self-contained Base64 Decoder
static int base64_decode(const char *in, size_t in_len, uint8_t *out,
                         size_t out_max, size_t *out_len) {
  size_t i = 0, j = 0;
  uint32_t val = 0;
  int valb = -8;
  for (i = 0; i < in_len; i++) {
    char c = in[i];
    if (c == '=')
      break;
    int d = -1;
    if (c >= 'A' && c <= 'Z')
      d = c - 'A';
    else if (c >= 'a' && c <= 'z')
      d = c - 'a' + 26;
    else if (c >= '0' && c <= '9')
      d = c - '0' + 52;
    else if (c == '+')
      d = 62;
    else if (c == '/')
      d = 63;
    if (d != -1) {
      val = (val << 6) | d;
      valb += 6;
      if (valb >= 0) {
        if (j >= out_max)
          return -1;
        out[j++] = (val >> valb) & 0xFF;
        valb -= 8;
      }
    }
  }
  *out_len = j;
  return 0;
}

// Maps encryption mode strings to integers used by libagora-rtc-sdk
static uint32_t get_encryption_mode_value(const char *mode_str) {
  if (!mode_str)
    return 8; // Default to AES_256_GCM2
  if (strcmp(mode_str, "AES_128_XTS") == 0)
    return 1;
  if (strcmp(mode_str, "AES_128_ECB") == 0)
    return 2;
  if (strcmp(mode_str, "AES_256_XTS") == 0)
    return 3;
  if (strcmp(mode_str, "SM4_128_ECB") == 0)
    return 4;
  if (strcmp(mode_str, "AES_128_GCM") == 0)
    return 5;
  if (strcmp(mode_str, "AES_256_GCM") == 0)
    return 6;
  if (strcmp(mode_str, "AES_128_GCM2") == 0)
    return 7;
  if (strcmp(mode_str, "AES_256_GCM2") == 0)
    return 8;

  char *endptr;
  unsigned long val = strtoul(mode_str, &endptr, 10);
  if (*endptr == '\0') {
    return (uint32_t)val;
  }
  return 0;
}

  if (size < 3) {

  for (size_t index = 0; index + 3 < size; ++index) {

      if (data[index + 2] == 0x01) {

          *code_len = 3;
        }
        return (int)index;

          data[index + 3] == 0x01) {
        if (code_len) {
          *code_len = 4;
        }
        return (int)index;
      }
    }
  }

I started with a 5V relay (CAT.NO: XC4419). I bought a 50 Watt PTC element heater off ebay, and a Keyes Temperature Detector Sensor With Module (CAT.NO: XC4653) from JayCar. 

<center><img src="/printer-upgrade/3.jpeg" alt="The two parallel 50 Watt PTC element heaters" width="60%"/></center>

<br />

I created a HTTP end point on the ESP32 which took a single variable as a query parameter to set the target temperature, if the temperature sensor ever rose above that temperature the relay switched the heater off.

<img src="/printer-upgrade/4.jpeg" alt="An image of a perf board with an ESP32 and a 5V relay" width="60%"/>

<br />

Next, I put a script on the printer to check if there was an active file being printed, if it was in ABS or another material which requires constant temperatures the printer would POST to the HTTP end point on the ESP32 and start the heater.

This was simple enough, it kept the temperatures comfortably at 30 degrees, but it's not hot enough, ABS ideally wants 60 degrees.

I added bubble foil and aluminium tape to double insulate the internal layers, _then to my wifes great joy_ I bought another 50W ebay heater and put it in parallel with the other one.

Now it reached 50 degrees stable.

That's probably enough for me, so I started printing.

...**ERROR**...

Oh dear, I ran into a problem I didn't foresee, something known as heat creep. 

Heat creep is... when the heat creeps... into places you don't want it. This was causing parts which should have been solid into liquids.

Easy fix, I'll just put a fan there and blow air onto it to cool it down.. but the whole printer was now 50 degrees, the air had to come from outside.

I ran some air lines from outside the printer into the chamber to blow air onto the motherboard and heat-break using a model I made in Fusion 360.

<center><img src="/printer-upgrade/6.jpg" alt="An air splitter modelled in Fusion 360" width="60%"/></center>

<br />

But I've never taken a fluid mechanics course, or a thermodynamics course, _I'm not a mechanical engineer_.

The blower fan I bought didn't have enough pressure to push the air through the tube, so I had to buy a CPAP.

A CPAP (or continuous positive air pressure) fan is essentially a blower designed for high RPM so it can maintain pressure.

The CPAP is extremely loud, so I printed a muffler for it and added an air filter and it brought the noise down to just above normal printing sound, if not somewhat more annoying.

The CPAP worked perfectly, heat creep was gone, the chamber was hot, printing was perfect.

<center><img src="/printer-upgrade/5.jpg" alt="An image of a CPAP with a muffler" width="60%"/></center>

<br />

So I added the CPAP to another 5V relay and stuck it to the ESP32, the CPAP using the same temperature sensor switches on automatically if the chamber is above 30 degrees.

I made it sound easy, but this took me all of my spare time for the better part of 5 weeks, <small><i>and cost a total of $400, mistakes included</i></small>. If I didn't make any mistakes the bill of materials would have totalled to $200.

Which isn't bad to double the strength of materials printed.

#include <DallasTemperature.h>
#include <ESPmDNS.h>
#include <FastLED.h>
#include <OneWire.h>
#include <Preferences.h>
#include <WiFi.h>

#define NUM_LEDS 1
#define DATA_PIN 48
#define HEATBREAK_COOLER_OUTPUT 5
#define RELAY_OUTPUT 6
#define ONE_WIRE_BUS 7

// WiFi provisioning
#define AP_SSID "anycubic-test-esp"
#define WIFI_CONNECT_TIMEOUT_MS 15000
#define MDNS_HOSTNAME "chamber-heater"

CRGB leds[NUM_LEDS];
OneWire oneWire(ONE_WIRE_BUS);
DallasTemperature sensors(&oneWire);
WiFiServer httpEndPoint(80);
Preferences prefs;

float currentTemperature = 0.0;
float targetTemperature = 0.0;
unsigned long printTime = 0;
unsigned long checkTempInterval = 500;
unsigned long lastCheckTemp = 0;
bool wifiConnected = false;
bool MANUAL_HEATBREAK_OVERRIDE = false;

// WiFi reconnection
unsigned long lastWifiCheck = 0;
const unsigned long WIFI_CHECK_INTERVAL_MS = 10000; // check every 10s

// URL-decode a string (handles %XX and + for space)
String urlDecode(const String &input) {
  String decoded = "";
  for (unsigned int i = 0; i < input.length(); i++) {
    char c = input.charAt(i);
    if (c == '%' && i + 2 < input.length()) {
      String hex = input.substring(i + 1, i + 3);
      decoded += (char)strtol(hex.c_str(), NULL, 16);
      i += 2;
    } else if (c == '+') {
      decoded += ' ';
    } else {
      decoded += c;
    }
  }
  return decoded;
}

// Extract a query parameter value from the raw HTTP request
String extractParam(const String &request, const String &paramName) {
  String searchKey = paramName + "=";
  int startIdx = request.indexOf(searchKey);
  if (startIdx == -1)
    return "";

  startIdx += searchKey.length();
  int endIdx = request.length();
  int spaceIdx = request.indexOf(" ", startIdx);
  int ampIdx = request.indexOf("&", startIdx);
  int newlineIdx = request.indexOf("\r", startIdx);

  if (spaceIdx != -1 && spaceIdx < endIdx)
    endIdx = spaceIdx;
  if (ampIdx != -1 && ampIdx < endIdx)
    endIdx = ampIdx;
  if (newlineIdx != -1 && newlineIdx < endIdx)
    endIdx = newlineIdx;

  String value = request.substring(startIdx, endIdx);
  value.trim();
  return urlDecode(value);
}

// Save WiFi credentials to persistent storage
void saveWiFiCredentials(const String &ssid, const String &password) {
  prefs.begin("wifi", false);
  prefs.putString("ssid", ssid);
  prefs.putString("password", password);
  prefs.end();
  Serial.println("Credentials saved to flash");
}

// Load WiFi credentials from persistent storage
bool loadWiFiCredentials(String &ssid, String &password) {
  prefs.begin("wifi", true);
  ssid = prefs.getString("ssid", "");
  password = prefs.getString("password", "");
  prefs.end();
  return ssid.length() > 0;
}

// Attempt to connect to WiFi with stored credentials
bool tryConnect() {
  String ssid, password;
  if (!loadWiFiCredentials(ssid, password)) {
    Serial.println("No stored credentials");
    return false;
  }

  Serial.printf("Connecting to '%s'...\n", ssid.c_str());
  leds[0] = CRGB::Blue;
  FastLED.show();

  WiFi.mode(WIFI_STA);
  WiFi.setHostname("test-esp");
  WiFi.begin(ssid.c_str(), password.c_str());

  unsigned long start = millis();
  while (WiFi.status() != WL_CONNECTED &&
         millis() - start < WIFI_CONNECT_TIMEOUT_MS) {
    delay(250);
  }

  if (WiFi.status() == WL_CONNECTED) {
    Serial.printf("Connected! IP: %s\n", WiFi.localIP().toString().c_str());
    return true;
  }

  Serial.println("Connection failed");
  WiFi.disconnect();
  return false;
}

// Start the AP for provisioning
void startAP() {
  Serial.println("Starting AP for provisioning...");
  leds[0] = CRGB::Yellow;
  FastLED.show();

  WiFi.mode(WIFI_AP);
  WiFi.softAP(AP_SSID); // open AP, no password
  delay(100);

  Serial.printf("AP '%s' started. IP: %s\n", AP_SSID,
                WiFi.softAPIP().toString().c_str());
}

// Handle the /wifi provisioning endpoint
// Returns true if credentials were received and a reboot is needed
bool handleWiFiEndpoint(WiFiClient &client, const String &request) {
  String newSSID = extractParam(request, "ssid");
  String newPassword = extractParam(request, "password");

  if (newSSID.length() == 0) {
    client.println("HTTP/1.1 400 Bad Request");
    client.println("Content-Type: text/plain");
    client.println("Connection: close");
    client.println();
    client.println("Missing 'ssid' parameter");
    return false;
  }

  saveWiFiCredentials(newSSID, newPassword);

  client.println("HTTP/1.1 200 OK");
  client.println("Content-Type: text/plain");
  client.println("Connection: close");
  client.println();
  client.printf("Credentials accepted for '%s'. Rebooting...\n",
                newSSID.c_str());
  client.flush();
  delay(100);
  client.stop();

  Serial.printf("Received credentials for '%s'. Rebooting in 1s...\n",
                newSSID.c_str());
  leds[0] = CRGB::Purple;
  FastLED.show();
  delay(1000);
  ESP.restart();

  return true; // never reached, but keeps compiler happy
}

// Handle the status / control endpoint (original functionality)
void handleStatusEndpoint(WiFiClient &client, const String &request) {
  // Parse target temperature
  String targetStr = extractParam(request, "target");
  if (targetStr.length() > 0) {
    targetTemperature = targetStr.toFloat();
  }

  // Parse timer
  String timerStr = extractParam(request, "timer");
  if (timerStr.length() > 0) {
    unsigned long timerSec = (unsigned long)timerStr.toInt();
    if (timerSec > 0) {      
      printTime = millis() + (timerSec * 1000UL) + (30UL * 60UL * 1000UL);
    } else {
      printTime = 0;
    }
  }

  String blowerStr = extractParam(request, "blower");
  if (blowerStr.length() > 0) {
    long blowerIsActivated = blowerStr.toInt();
    if (blowerIsActivated == 0) {
      MANUAL_HEATBREAK_OVERRIDE = false;
    } else if (blowerIsActivated == 1) {
      MANUAL_HEATBREAK_OVERRIDE = true;
    }
  }

  // Send the response
  client.println("HTTP/1.1 200 OK");
  client.println("Content-Type: text/plain");
  client.println("Connection: close");
  client.println();
  client.print("Time Remaining: ");
  if (printTime > 0 && millis() < printTime) {
    client.print((printTime - millis()) / 1000UL);
  } else {
    client.print("0");
  }
  client.println(" S");
  client.print("Current Temp: ");
  client.print(currentTemperature);
  client.println(" C");
  client.print("Target Temp: ");
  client.print(targetTemperature);
  client.println(" C");
  client.print("Relay: "); // we return an estimate of the relays state based on
                           // target temp, the actual state is irrelevant
  if (digitalRead(RELAY_OUTPUT) == HIGH) {
    client.println("ON");
  } else {
    client.println("OFF");
  }
}

void setup() {
  Serial.begin(115200);

  while (!Serial && millis() < 4000) {
    delay(10);
  }

  pinMode(RELAY_OUTPUT, OUTPUT);
  pinMode(HEATBREAK_COOLER_OUTPUT, OUTPUT);
  digitalWrite(RELAY_OUTPUT, LOW); // relay off at start
  digitalWrite(HEATBREAK_COOLER_OUTPUT, LOW);
  sensors.begin();
  sensors.setWaitForConversion(false);
  FastLED.addLeds<NEOPIXEL, DATA_PIN>(leds, NUM_LEDS);
  FastLED.clear();

  // Try connecting with stored credentials
  wifiConnected = tryConnect();

  if (!wifiConnected) {
    // No credentials or connection failed — start AP for provisioning
    startAP();
  } else {
    WiFi.setAutoReconnect(true);
    // Start mDNS on successful connection
    if (!MDNS.begin(MDNS_HOSTNAME)) {
      Serial.println("mDNS setup failed");
    }
  }

  // Start HTTP server in both modes (STA for normal use, AP for provisioning)
  httpEndPoint.begin();

  if (wifiConnected) {
    leds[0] = CRGB::Green;
  }
  FastLED.show();
}

void loop() {
  // WiFi reconnection check (only in STA mode)
  if (wifiConnected && millis() - lastWifiCheck >= WIFI_CHECK_INTERVAL_MS) {
    lastWifiCheck = millis();
    if (WiFi.status() != WL_CONNECTED) {
      Serial.println("WiFi lost — attempting reconnect...");
      leds[0] = CRGB::Blue;
      FastLED.show();
      WiFi.reconnect();
    } else {
      // Connection restored (or was never lost)
      if (leds[0] != CRGB::Green) {
        Serial.printf("WiFi reconnected. IP: %s\n",
                      WiFi.localIP().toString().c_str());
        leds[0] = CRGB::Green;
        FastLED.show();
        // Re-init mDNS after reconnection
        MDNS.end();
        if (!MDNS.begin(MDNS_HOSTNAME)) {
          Serial.println("mDNS re-init failed");
          leds[0] = CRGB::Red;
          FastLED.show();
        }
      }
    }
  }

  // Print timer expiry
  if (printTime > 0 && millis() >= printTime) {
    targetTemperature = 0.0;
    printTime = 0;
  }


  // Temperature polling
  if ((unsigned long)(millis() - lastCheckTemp) > checkTempInterval) {
    sensors.requestTemperatures();
    float rawTemp = sensors.getTempCByIndex(0);

    // Filter out OneWire fault codes (-127 and 85 are standard DS18B20 error codes)
    if (rawTemp > -50.0 && rawTemp < 125.0 && rawTemp != 85.0) {
      currentTemperature = rawTemp;
    } else {
      Serial.println("Warning: DS18B20 read error ignored!");
    }
    lastCheckTemp = millis();
  }

  // Relay control
  if (targetTemperature < 40) {
    digitalWrite(RELAY_OUTPUT, LOW);
  } else {
    if (printTime > 0) {
      if (currentTemperature <= (targetTemperature - 2.5)) {
        digitalWrite(RELAY_OUTPUT, HIGH);
      } else if (currentTemperature >= (targetTemperature + 2.5)) {
        digitalWrite(RELAY_OUTPUT, LOW);
      }
    } else {
      digitalWrite(RELAY_OUTPUT, LOW);
    }
  }
  
  if (MANUAL_HEATBREAK_OVERRIDE) {
    digitalWrite(HEATBREAK_COOLER_OUTPUT, HIGH);
  } else if (currentTemperature >= 30.0) {
    digitalWrite(HEATBREAK_COOLER_OUTPUT, HIGH);
  } else if (currentTemperature < 27.0) {
    digitalWrite(HEATBREAK_COOLER_OUTPUT, LOW);
  }

  // HTTP request handling
  WiFiClient client = httpEndPoint.available();
  if (client) {
    String fullRequest = "";
    unsigned long timeout = millis();

    // Read full request (headers + body)
    while (client.connected() && millis() - timeout < 500) {
      while (client.available()) {
        char c = client.read();
        fullRequest += c;
        timeout = millis();
      }
    }

    // Route: /wifi endpoint for provisioning
    if (fullRequest.startsWith("GET /wifi")) {
      handleWiFiEndpoint(client, fullRequest);
      return; // ESP will reboot, never reaches here
    }

    // Route: everything else is the status/control endpoint (original
    // behaviour)
    handleStatusEndpoint(client, fullRequest);

    delay(10);
    client.stop();
  }
}

import socket
import struct
import time
import logging

MDNS_ADDR = "224.0.0.251"
MDNS_PORT = 5353
CACHE_FILE = "/tmp/chamber_heater_ip"
PERSISTENT_CACHE = "/userdata/chamber_heater_ip"
TARGET = "chamber-heater.local"
REFRESH_INTERVAL = 60
QUERY_TIMEOUT = 5.0

logging.basicConfig(level=logging.INFO, format="%(asctime)s %(levelname)s %(message)s")
log = logging.getLogger("mdns_resolve")

def encode_dns_name(name):
    parts = name.encode("ascii").split(b".")
    result = b""
    for part in parts:
        result += struct.pack("B", len(part)) + part
    result += b"\x00"
    return result

def parse_dns_name(data, offset):
    labels = []
    original_offset = None
    while offset < len(data):
        length = data[offset]
        if length == 0:
            offset += 1
            break
        if (length & 0xC0) == 0xC0:
            if original_offset is None:
                original_offset = offset + 2
            pointer = struct.unpack("!H", data[offset:offset+2])[0] & 0x3FFF
            offset = pointer
            continue
        offset += 1
        labels.append(data[offset:offset+length].decode("ascii", errors="replace"))
        offset += length
    return ".".join(labels), (original_offset if original_offset is not None else offset)

def build_query(name):
    header = struct.pack("!HHHHHH", 0, 0, 1, 0, 0, 0)
    encoded = encode_dns_name(name)
    question = encoded + struct.pack("!HH", 1, 0x8001)
    return header + question

def parse_response(data, target):
    if len(data) < 12:
        return None
    flags = struct.unpack("!H", data[2:4])[0]
    if not (flags & 0x8000):
        return None
    ancount = struct.unpack("!H", data[6:8])[0]
    if ancount == 0:
        return None
    offset = 12
    qdcount = struct.unpack("!H", data[4:6])[0]
    for _ in range(qdcount):
        if offset >= len(data):
            return None
        _, offset = parse_dns_name(data, offset)
        offset += 4
    for _ in range(ancount):
        if offset >= len(data):
            return None
        name, offset = parse_dns_name(data, offset)
        if offset + 10 > len(data):
            return None
        rtype, rclass, ttl, rdlen = struct.unpack("!HHIH", data[offset:offset+10])
        offset += 10
        if (name.lower() == target.lower() and rtype == 1 and (rclass & 0x7FFF) == 1 and rdlen == 4):
            return socket.inet_ntoa(data[offset:offset+4])
        offset += rdlen
    return None

def resolve_once(target):
    query = build_query(target)
    sock = socket.socket(socket.AF_INET, socket.SOCK_DGRAM, socket.IPPROTO_UDP)
    sock.setsockopt(socket.IPPROTO_IP, socket.IP_MULTICAST_TTL, 255)
    sock.settimeout(QUERY_TIMEOUT)
    try:
        sock.sendto(query, (MDNS_ADDR, MDNS_PORT))
        deadline = time.monotonic() + QUERY_TIMEOUT
        while time.monotonic() < deadline:
            try:
                data, _ = sock.recvfrom(1500)
                ip = parse_response(data, target)
                if ip:
                    return ip
            except socket.timeout:
                break
    finally:
        sock.close()
    return None

def write_cache(ip):
    for path in [CACHE_FILE, PERSISTENT_CACHE]:
        try:
            with open(path, "w") as f:
                f.write(ip)
        except Exception as e:
            log.warning("Could not write cache %s: %s", path, e)
    log.info("Resolved %s -> %s", TARGET, ip)

def read_cache():
    for path in [CACHE_FILE, PERSISTENT_CACHE]:
        try:
            with open(path) as f:
                ip = f.read().strip()
                if ip:
                    return ip
        except Exception:
            continue
    return None

def main():
    log.info("Starting mDNS resolver for %s", TARGET)
    last_success = None
    while True:
        ip = resolve_once(TARGET)
        if ip:
            if ip != last_success:
                write_cache(ip)
                last_success = ip
        else:
            if last_success is None and read_cache() is None:
                log.warning("No cached IP available for %s", TARGET)
        time.sleep(REFRESH_INTERVAL)

if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()

import sys

import json

DEBUG_LOG = "/tmp/chamber_debug.log"

def log_debug(message):
    try:
        timestamp = datetime.now().strftime('[%Y-%m-%d %H:%M:%S]')
        with open(DEBUG_LOG, "a") as f:
            f.write(f"{timestamp} {message}\n")
    except Exception as e:
        print(f"Error writing to log: {e}", file=sys.stderr)

def get_valid_ip():
    for cache_path in [RUNTIME_CACHE, PERSISTENT_CACHE]:
        if os.path.exists(cache_path):
            try:
                with open(cache_path, "r") as f:
                    ip = f.read().strip()
                if ip and ip != "0.0.0.0":
                    return ip
            except Exception:
                pass
    return None

def parse_gcode_file(filename):
    full_path = os.path.join(GCODES_BASE, filename)
    if not os.path.isfile(full_path):
        log_debug(f"[DEBUG_ERR] File not found on disk: [{full_path}]")
        return None, None

    raw_target = 0
    total_estimate = 1800

    try:
        # Read the first 128KB for M141
        with open(full_path, "rb") as f:
            read_size = min(os.path.getsize(full_path), 131072)
            first_chunk = f.read(read_size).decode("utf-8", errors="ignore")

            for line in first_chunk.splitlines():
                if "M141" in line:
                    match = re.search(r'M141.*?S(\d+)', line)
                    if match:
                        raw_target = int(match.group(1))
                        break

        # Read last 128KB of the file for the print time estimate
        try:
            with open(full_path, "rb") as f:
                f.seek(0, os.SEEK_END)
                size = f.tell()
                read_size = min(size, 131072)
                f.seek(-read_size, os.SEEK_END)
                last_chunk = f.read(read_size).decode("utf-8", errors="ignore")
                
                time_match = re.search(r'estimated printing time \(normal mode\)\s*=\s*(.*)', last_chunk, re.IGNORECASE)
                if time_match:
                    time_str = time_match.group(1).strip()
                    h = re.search(r'(\d+)h', time_str)
                    m = re.search(r'(\d+)m', time_str)
                    s_match = re.search(r'(\d+)s', time_str)
                    total_estimate = (int(h.group(1)) * 3600 if h else 0) + \
                                     (int(m.group(1)) * 60 if m else 0) + \
                                     (int(s_match.group(1)) if s_match else 0)
        except Exception as e:
            log_debug(f"[DEBUG_ERR] Error parsing end of gcode file: {e}")

    except Exception as e:
        log_debug(f"[DEBUG_ERR] Error reading gcode file: {e}")

    return raw_target, total_estimate

def run_monitor_loop():

    last_file = ""

    while True:

        except Exception as e:

            continue

        if state in ["printing", "paused"]:
            if current_file:

                if raw_target is not None:

                    if active_ip:

                            log_debug(f"[DEBUG_LOOP] Polled State: [{state}] | Required: [{raw_target}] | ESP32 Target: [{live_hardware_target}] | GCode Estimate: [{total_estimate}] | Remaining: [{time_remaining}] | Elapsed: [{duration}]")

                                    log_debug("[DEBUG_CURL_RESULT] Correction status: [0]")
                                except Exception as curl_err:

                    else:

        elif state in ["standby", "cancelled", "complete"]:
            if last_file:
                log_debug("[DEBUG_SHUTDOWN] Print ended. Disabling heater relays.")
                last_file = ""
                active_ip = get_valid_ip()
                if active_ip:
                    try:
                        shutdown_url = f"http://{active_ip}/?target=0"
                        with urllib.request.urlopen(shutdown_url, timeout=4) as _:
                            pass
                    except Exception as shutdown_err:
                        log_debug(f"[DEBUG_ERR] Failed to send shutdown command to ESP32: {shutdown_err}")

        time.sleep(5)

if __name__ == "__main__":
    run_monitor_loop()

      #!/usr/bin/env python3
"""
ESP32 WiFi Auto-Connector

Scans for the ESP32 chamber heater's fallback AP ('test-esp') and, when found,
sends the printer's WiFi credentials to the ESP32 so it can join the home network.
Reads credentials from /userdata/wifi_cfg/passwd_history.cfg.

This eliminates the need to manually connect a phone to configure the ESP32.
"""
import os
import sys
import json
import time
import subprocess
import urllib.request
import urllib.parse
import urllib.error
import socket
from datetime import datetime

# --- Configuration ---
ESP32_AP_SSID = "anycubic-test-esp"
ESP32_AP_IP = "192.168.4.1"
ESP32_WIFI_ENDPOINT = "/wifi"
PASSWD_HISTORY = "/userdata/wifi_cfg/passwd_history.cfg"
WPA_INTERFACE = "wlan0"
SCAN_INTERVAL = 30       # seconds between scans when ESP32 not detected
PROVISION_COOLDOWN = 120  # seconds to wait after a provisioning attempt
DEBUG_LOG = "/tmp/esp32_connector.log"
MAX_LOG_SIZE = 512 * 1024  # 512KB, rotate when exceeded

# --- Logging ---
def log(message):
    try:
        timestamp = datetime.now().strftime("[%Y-%m-%d %H:%M:%S]")
        line = f"{timestamp} {message}\n"
        # Rotate log if too large
        if os.path.exists(DEBUG_LOG):
            try:
                if os.path.getsize(DEBUG_LOG) > MAX_LOG_SIZE:
                    os.rename(DEBUG_LOG, DEBUG_LOG + ".old")
            except OSError:
                pass
        with open(DEBUG_LOG, "a") as f:
            f.write(line)
    except Exception:
        pass

## --- Display (fb_draw) ---
try:
    import fb_draw
    _fb_available = True
except ImportError:
    _fb_available = False

def display(message, color="green", save=True, timeout=None):
    """
    Show a status message on the printer's LCD using fb_draw.
    Gracefully does nothing if fb_draw fails or isn't available.
    """
    if not _fb_available:
        return

    color_map = {
        "cyan": (0, 255, 255),
        "yellow": (255, 255, 0),
        "red": (255, 0, 0),
        "green": (0, 255, 0),
    }
    rgb = color_map.get(color, (255, 255, 255))
    
    fb_draw.fb_show(message, color=rgb)
    
    if timeout is not None:
        # Note: the original fb_status did auto-hide, but here we just leave it
        # on the screen for 'timeout' seconds since our python script runs synchronously.
        # Actually, since this is called during blocking steps, we shouldn't sleep here unless
        # it's just a message. Wait, the caller handles sleep for success/fail messages.
        pass

def display_hide():
    """Restore the original screen content."""
    if _fb_available:
        fb_draw.fb_hide()

# --- WiFi scanning ---
def scan_for_ssid(target_ssid):
    """
    Scan for a specific SSID using available WiFi tools.
    Returns True if the target SSID is visible, False otherwise.
    """
    # Try wpa_cli first (most common on embedded Linux)
    try:
        subprocess.run(
            ["wpa_cli", "-i", WPA_INTERFACE, "scan"],
            capture_output=True, timeout=10
        )
        # Wait for scan to complete
        time.sleep(3)
        result = subprocess.run(
            ["wpa_cli", "-i", WPA_INTERFACE, "scan_results"],
            capture_output=True, text=True, timeout=10
        )
        if result.returncode == 0:
            for line in result.stdout.splitlines():
                # wpa_cli scan_results format: bssid / freq / signal / flags / ssid
                parts = line.split("\t")
                if len(parts) >= 5 and parts[4].strip() == target_ssid:
                    log(f"[SCAN] Found '{target_ssid}' via wpa_cli (signal: {parts[2]})")
                    return True
            return False
    except (FileNotFoundError, subprocess.TimeoutExpired):
        pass

    # Fallback: try iwlist
    try:
        result = subprocess.run(
            ["iwlist", WPA_INTERFACE, "scan"],
            capture_output=True, text=True, timeout=15
        )
        if result.returncode == 0 and target_ssid in result.stdout:
            log(f"[SCAN] Found '{target_ssid}' via iwlist")
            return True
        return False
    except (FileNotFoundError, subprocess.TimeoutExpired):
        pass

    # Fallback: try iw
    try:
        result = subprocess.run(
            ["iw", "dev", WPA_INTERFACE, "scan"],
            capture_output=True, text=True, timeout=15
        )
        if result.returncode == 0 and target_ssid in result.stdout:
            log(f"[SCAN] Found '{target_ssid}' via iw")
            return True
        return False
    except (FileNotFoundError, subprocess.TimeoutExpired):
        pass

    log("[SCAN] No WiFi scan tools available (tried wpa_cli, iwlist, iw)")
    return False

# --- Credential loading ---
def load_printer_credentials():
    """
    Load WiFi credentials from the printer's password history file.
    Returns (ssid, password) of the currently connected network, or the first entry.
    """
    if not os.path.exists(PASSWD_HISTORY):
        log(f"[CREDS] Password history not found: {PASSWD_HISTORY}")
        return None, None

    try:
        with open(PASSWD_HISTORY, "r") as f:
            data = json.load(f)
    except (json.JSONDecodeError, IOError) as e:
        log(f"[CREDS] Failed to parse {PASSWD_HISTORY}: {e}")
        return None, None

    wifi_list = data.get("wifi", [])
    if not wifi_list:
        log("[CREDS] No WiFi entries in password history")
        return None, None

    # Try to find the currently connected SSID
    current_ssid = get_current_ssid()
    if current_ssid:
        for entry in wifi_list:
            if entry.get("ssid") == current_ssid:
                log(f"[CREDS] Matched current SSID: '{current_ssid}'")
                return entry["ssid"], entry.get("passwd", "")

    # Fallback: use the first entry
    entry = wifi_list[0]
    log(f"[CREDS] Using first entry: '{entry.get('ssid', '?')}'")
    return entry.get("ssid", ""), entry.get("passwd", "")


def get_current_ssid():
    """Get the SSID the printer is currently connected to."""
    try:
        result = subprocess.run(
            ["wpa_cli", "-i", WPA_INTERFACE, "status"],
            capture_output=True, text=True, timeout=5
        )
        if result.returncode == 0:
            for line in result.stdout.splitlines():
                if line.startswith("ssid="):
                    return line.split("=", 1)[1].strip()
    except (FileNotFoundError, subprocess.TimeoutExpired):
        pass

    # Fallback: try iwconfig
    try:
        result = subprocess.run(
            ["iwconfig", WPA_INTERFACE],
            capture_output=True, text=True, timeout=5
        )
        if result.returncode == 0:
            for line in result.stdout.splitlines():
                if "ESSID:" in line:
                    # Extract ESSID:"NetworkName"
                    start = line.index('ESSID:"') + 7
                    end = line.index('"', start)
                    return line[start:end]
    except (FileNotFoundError, subprocess.TimeoutExpired, ValueError):
        pass

    return None

# --- Network switching ---
def save_current_network():
    """Save info about the current network so we can reconnect later."""
    ssid = get_current_ssid()
    if ssid:
        log(f"[NET] Saved current network: '{ssid}'")
    return ssid


def connect_to_ap(ssid):
    """
    Connect the printer to the ESP32's open AP using wpa_cli.
    Returns True on success.
    """
    log(f"[NET] Connecting to AP '{ssid}'...")

    try:
        # Add a temporary network
        result = subprocess.run(
            ["wpa_cli", "-i", WPA_INTERFACE, "add_network"],
            capture_output=True, text=True, timeout=5
        )
        network_id = result.stdout.strip().splitlines()[-1]

        # Set SSID
        subprocess.run(
            ["wpa_cli", "-i", WPA_INTERFACE, "set_network", network_id, "ssid", f'"{ssid}"'],
            capture_output=True, timeout=5
        )

        # Set key_mgmt to NONE (open AP)
        subprocess.run(
            ["wpa_cli", "-i", WPA_INTERFACE, "set_network", network_id, "key_mgmt", "NONE"],
            capture_output=True, timeout=5
        )

        # Select this network (disconnects from current)
        subprocess.run(
            ["wpa_cli", "-i", WPA_INTERFACE, "select_network", network_id],
            capture_output=True, timeout=5
        )

        # Wait for connection
        for attempt in range(15):
            time.sleep(1)
            status = subprocess.run(
                ["wpa_cli", "-i", WPA_INTERFACE, "status"],
                capture_output=True, text=True, timeout=5
            )
            for line in status.stdout.splitlines():
                if line.startswith("wpa_state=") and "COMPLETED" in line:
                    log(f"[NET] Connected to AP '{ssid}' (attempt {attempt + 1})")
                    # Force a static IP so we can route to 192.168.4.1 instantly without killing system DHCP
                    log(f"[NET] Setting static IP 192.168.4.2...")
                    subprocess.run(["ifconfig", WPA_INTERFACE, "192.168.4.2", "netmask", "255.255.255.0", "up"], capture_output=True)
                    time.sleep(1)
                    return True

        log(f"[NET] Failed to connect to AP '{ssid}' after 15 attempts")
        return False

    except (FileNotFoundError, subprocess.TimeoutExpired) as e:
        log(f"[NET] wpa_cli error connecting to AP: {e}")
        return False


def reconnect_home_network():
    """
    Reconnect to the home WiFi by telling wpa_supplicant to reconfigure.
    This reloads the original wpa_supplicant.conf networks.
    """
    log("[NET] Reconnecting to home network...")
    try:
        # Reconfigure reloads wpa_supplicant.conf and reconnects to known networks
        subprocess.run(
            ["wpa_cli", "-i", WPA_INTERFACE, "reconfigure"],
            capture_output=True, timeout=10
        )
        # Wait for reconnection
        for attempt in range(20):
            time.sleep(1)
            status = subprocess.run(
                ["wpa_cli", "-i", WPA_INTERFACE, "status"],
                capture_output=True, text=True, timeout=5
            )
            for line in status.stdout.splitlines():
                if line.startswith("wpa_state=") and "COMPLETED" in line:
                    ssid = get_current_ssid()
                    log(f"[NET] Reconnected to home network '{ssid}' (attempt {attempt + 1})")
                    log(f"[NET] Clearing static IP, relying on system DHCP...")
                    subprocess.run(["ifconfig", WPA_INTERFACE, "0.0.0.0"], capture_output=True)
                    time.sleep(2)
                    return True

        log("[NET] Failed to reconnect to home network after 20 attempts")
        return False

    except (FileNotFoundError, subprocess.TimeoutExpired) as e:
        log(f"[NET] wpa_cli error reconnecting: {e}")
        return False

# --- ESP32 provisioning ---
def send_credentials_to_esp32(wifi_ssid, wifi_password):
    """
    Send WiFi credentials to the ESP32 via its /wifi HTTP endpoint.
    The ESP32 should be reachable at 192.168.4.1 when we're connected to its AP.
    """
    params = urllib.parse.urlencode({
        "ssid": wifi_ssid,
        "password": wifi_password
    })
    url = f"http://{ESP32_AP_IP}{ESP32_WIFI_ENDPOINT}?{params}"

    log(f"[PROV] Sending credentials to ESP32: ssid='{wifi_ssid}'")

    try:
        req = urllib.request.Request(url)
        with urllib.request.urlopen(req, timeout=10) as response:
            body = response.read().decode("utf-8", errors="ignore")
            log(f"[PROV] ESP32 response: {body.strip()}")
            return True
    except urllib.error.URLError as e:
        log(f"[PROV] HTTP error sending credentials: {e}")
        return False
    except socket.timeout:
        log("[PROV] Timeout sending credentials to ESP32")
        return False
    except Exception as e:
        log(f"[PROV] Unexpected error: {e}")
        return False

# --- Main provisioning flow ---
def provision_esp32():
    """
    Full provisioning sequence:
    1. Read WiFi creds from printer's password history
    2. Save current network state
    3. Connect to ESP32's AP
    4. Send credentials
    5. Reconnect to home network
    """
    log("[PROV] === Starting provisioning sequence ===")
    display("Chamber Heater\nESP32 detected...", color="cyan")

    # Step 1: Load credentials
    wifi_ssid, wifi_password = load_printer_credentials()
    if not wifi_ssid:
        log("[PROV] No credentials available. Aborting.")
        display("Chamber Heater\nNo WiFi credentials found", color="red", timeout=5)
        return False

    # Step 2: Save current network
    home_ssid = save_current_network()

    # Step 3: Connect to ESP32 AP
    display(f"Chamber Heater\nConnecting to ESP32...", color="yellow")
    if not connect_to_ap(ESP32_AP_SSID):
        log("[PROV] Could not connect to ESP32 AP. Reconnecting to home...")
        display("Chamber Heater\nConnection failed", color="red", timeout=5)
        reconnect_home_network()
        return False

    # Step 4: Send credentials
    display(f"Chamber Heater\nSending credentials...", color="yellow")
    success = send_credentials_to_esp32(wifi_ssid, wifi_password)

    # Step 5: Reconnect to home network (always, regardless of success)
    display(f"Chamber Heater\nReconnecting...", color="yellow")
    time.sleep(2)  # Brief pause to let ESP32 process
    reconnect_home_network()

    if success:
        log("[PROV] === Provisioning complete! ESP32 will reboot and join the network ===")
        display("Chamber Heater\nProvisioning complete!", color="green", timeout=8)
    else:
        log("[PROV] === Provisioning failed. Will retry on next scan ===")
        display("Chamber Heater\nProvisioning failed", color="red", timeout=8)

    # Restore the original screen after the timeout message clears
    time.sleep(9)
    display_hide()

    return success

# --- Main loop ---
def main():
    log("[MAIN] ESP32 WiFi Connector started")
    log(f"[MAIN] Scanning for '{ESP32_AP_SSID}' every {SCAN_INTERVAL}s")
    if _fb_available:
        log("[MAIN] fb_draw available for UI overlays")
    else:
        log("[MAIN] fb_draw not available — display messages disabled")

    while True:
        try:
            if scan_for_ssid(ESP32_AP_SSID):
                log(f"[MAIN] ESP32 AP '{ESP32_AP_SSID}' detected! Starting provisioning...")
                provision_esp32()
                # Cooldown after provisioning attempt (give ESP32 time to reboot)
                log(f"[MAIN] Cooling down for {PROVISION_COOLDOWN}s...")
                time.sleep(PROVISION_COOLDOWN)
            else:
                # ESP32 is either connected to WiFi or not powered on — all good
                time.sleep(SCAN_INTERVAL)

        except Exception as e:
            log(f"[MAIN] Unexpected error in main loop: {e}")
            display_hide()  # Ensure screen is restored on error
            time.sleep(SCAN_INTERVAL)


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()
