# Logistics

At the start of World War 2, in 1939, there were approximately 565,000 Jews living in Germany.

All together in central Europe there were approximately 5 million Jews, this is what would largely be called "Nazi" controlled area during World War 2. Source: [Holocaust Museum](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/jewish-population-of-europe-in-1933-population-data-by-country)

The distance between Berlin, Germany and Auschwitz, Poland is about 600km, and takes 8 hours by train.

The Sydney metro line can accommodate about 1,000 people per train.

If we assume these numbers then we can accommodate 1,000 people per train, then it would take 6,000 trains to transport them all.

```
y=6,000,000 people ÷ 1,000 people per train
y=6,000 trains
```

### Synchronous

It follows that if this is the average distance from any Jew to Auschwitz, then 6,000 trains running at 8 hours per train, would take 48,000 hours to transport 6 million Jews (assuming 1 train at a time).

```
y=6,000 trains x 8 hours per train
y=48,000 hours
```

One year has 8,760 hours. So we can divide 48,000 hours by 8,760 to get the number of years it would take to transport 6 million Jews. Approximately 5.5 years.

```
y=48,000 hours ÷ 8,760 hours per year
y=5.479 years
```

### Asynchronous

Trains don't run one at a time, we have multiple trains running. 

We need to squash 48,000 hours into 4 years worth of hours.

```
y=4*365*24
y=35,000 hours

z=48,000 hours ÷ 35,000 hours
z=1.37 trains needed
```

So we have 1.37 trains running without stopping.

A train with 1,000 Jews will arrive every 17 hours.

Or 3,287 Jews per day per train.

or 1,200,000 Jews per year.

# Extermination

The Nazis had 6 death camps.

There were 52 crematoriums across the camps.

### Crematoriums

It takes about 90 minutes to cremate a body. 

If we were to cremate 6 million bodies in 52 crematoriums it would take 6,000,000 ÷ 52 = 115,384 hours.

115,384 hours ÷ 8,760 hours per year = 13.2 years.

To finish in 4 years, we need 171 crematoriums.

We're at about a third of our capacity. Considering they were collecting Jews and we assume they intended to exterminate them (on track to finish in 5 or 6 years), it is an odd number of crematoriums to have, why didn't they have more?

If they were aiming to save costs, it's far less efficient to use open pits than crematoriums due to heat loss.

### Coke

It takes about 90 kilograms of coke to cremate a body.

If we were to cremate 6 million bodies in 52 crematoriums it would take `6,000,000 x 90 =` 540,000,000 kilograms of coke.

Official documents released by the Soviets after the liberartion of Auschwitz note that between Feb 1942 and October 1943 there was 497 tonnes of coke delivered to Auschwitz. Source: [Debunking holocaust denial](https://www.hdot.org/debunking-denial/ab7-coke-coal-cremation/#_edn4)

We can assume this as the average delivery speed and approximate the remaining years.

```
y=4 x 12 months
y=48 months
```

Feb 1942 to Oct 1943 is 21 months.

Average the delivery to per month.

```
y=497/21
y=23 tonnes per month delivered
```

Calculate the total coke delivered in 4 year from 1941 to 1945.

```
y=23 tonnes * 48 months
y=1,104 tonnes
```

So at the rates given to us by the Soviets from official camp documents, we can estimate that 1,104 tonnes were delivered.

We know we need about 540,000 tonnes, lets calculate how much we needed, or _we must assume were delivered outside of the range of the documents_.

```
z=540,000 - 1,104 tonnes = 538,896 tonnes
```

We're short about 538,000 tonnes of coke required for the capacity needed to cremate 6 million. 

The density of coke is about 1.3 grams per cubic centimeter.

Just for curiosity sake, we can calculate how much space would be needed to store 540,000 tonnes.

```
y=540,000,000kg * 1000g/kg / 1.3g/cm
y=415,384,615,384 cubic centimeters
```

That’s a huge volume — about the size of 170 Olympic swimming pools.

From aerial photos, there is no evidence of massive coke storage.

There's also no reports from camp survivors or Allies who liberated or surveilled the area of mass coke storage.

### Fingernails in the walls

It's said that as Jews were being gassed to death, they clawed at the walls and left scratch marks. 

For a scratch to occur, you need enough force to cause permanent plastic deformation. 

A material can only scratch another if it can generate contact stresses exceeding the other material’s yield strength without itself plastically deforming first.

Stress is defined as `s=F/A`.

The hardness of concrete is often defined as `H=3s`, where H is can be measured by the Rockwell Hardness test. 

Typical values range 0.5-1.5 GPa conservatively, we assume 1 GPa.

Stress (`s`) is then equal to around 0.33 GPa.

For a finger nail to cause enough stress to exceed the yield strength of concrete, it would need to be able to generate a force greater than 330 MPa.

Is that possible?

If the area of a finger nail is 2cm long by 0.5cm wide, then it follows that the force required is about 33,300 Newtons.

If you weighed 70kg, your standing force is about 700 Newtons.

If we hand wave our calculations, you can see that if you push at a wall with your standing force with a finger nail, then you're still 32,600 Newtons short of scratching the walls of the gas chamber.

Even if we do our best to assume you can push at a wall with the acceleration of 100m/s^2, an order of magnitude greater than gravity, then we're still tens of thousands of Newtons away from the proposed force required.
